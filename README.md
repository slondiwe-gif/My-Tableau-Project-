# 📊 Tableau Project – Global Health & Retail Insights  
**Data Technician Bootcamp – Project**

This repository contains a collection of **interactive Tableau dashboards** created during my Data Technician bootcamp. The project demonstrates my ability to analyse real‑world datasets, design multi‑view dashboards, apply filters and calculated fields, and communicate insights through clear, structured data storytelling.

### 🔹 What I Found  
> *“Europe has the highest life expectancy, while Africa and parts of Asia show lower averages. Population distribution shows that larger populations may require more resources and place additional pressure on healthcare systems. The pie chart demonstrates an almost 50/50 gender split, although proportions differ between countries. The scatter plot suggests that countries with higher life expectancy tend to have moderate to high BMI levels, indicating BMI alone does not determine health outcomes.”*

### 🔹 How the NHS Could Use This  
> *“The NHS can use this information to implement preventative measures by analysing countries with higher life expectancy and better health outcomes. It can also target areas with lower life expectancy and provide educational resources and additional funding. The dashboard supports evidence‑based decisions, preventative strategies for obesity, promoting healthy lifestyles, and improving long‑term health across populations.”*
