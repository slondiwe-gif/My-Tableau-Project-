# 📊 Tableau Project – Interactive Retail & Global Health Dashboards  
**Data Technician Bootcamp **

This repository showcases a collection of **interactive Tableau dashboards** created during my Data Technician bootcamp. The project demonstrates my ability to transform raw datasets into clear, meaningful insights using strong data‑visualisation techniques and data storytelling.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview  
Across multiple Tableau worksheets and dashboards, I explored two datasets:

1. **Gapminder Global Health Data** – analysing life expectancy, population trends, BMI, and health metrics across continents.  
2. **Spotify Retail & Sales Data** – exploring genre trends, track popularity, and audio features to understand consumer behaviour.

These dashboards were combined into interactive visual stories designed to help organisations make evidence‑based decisions.

---

## 🧠 Key Skills Demonstrated  

### **🔹 Interactive Dashboards**
Built multi‑sheet dashboards with dynamic interactions, enabling users to explore data by country, gender, year, genre, or metric.

### **🔹 Filters, Slicers & Controls**
Used:
- Single‑value dropdown filters  
- Global filters across multiple worksheets  
- Interactive slicers for deeper exploration  
- Dashboard‑level controls for user‑driven analysis  

### **🔹 Calculated Fields**
Created custom calculations such as:
- Percentage of total (population distribution)  
- Average life expectancy  
- Trend metrics  
- Custom aggregations for retail and music analytics  

### **🔹 Variety of Chart Types**
Developed a wide range of visuals, including:
- **Bar charts** – Life expectancy by continent  
- **Line charts** – Trends over time  
- **Pie charts** – Gender population distribution  
- **Scatter plots** – BMI vs life expectancy  
- **Maps** – Geographic insights  
- **Custom charts** for Spotify audio features  

### **🔹 Data Storytelling**
Interpreted key insights such as:
- Differences in life expectancy across continents  
- Gender‑based population distribution  
- Relationship between BMI and health outcomes  
- Retail/music popularity trends across genres  

These insights demonstrate how visual analytics can support organisations like the NHS, retailers, or music platforms in making strategic, data‑driven decisions.
